# Marsupial Mayhem™

## ▶ [Play it now in your browser](https://marknakasone85.github.io/marsupial-mayhem/)

A comical cartoon brawler starring marsupials. Kangaroos, wombats, koalas, possums and friends battle it out in the Great Pouch Tournament, until uninvited pests crash the party.

**The whole adventure is playable:** 12 marsupials (plus 2 secret ones to find) and all 7 worlds of the **Marsupial World** map (25 maps, plus a bonus trip up to the Top End around Darwin and a secret bonus map). Pick your marsupial and walk the map from the Outback through the Rainforest, the Cane Fields & Coast, Tasmania & the Alps and the City, with a bonus trip through a Time Rift, all the way to the grand finale on Grin Isle. Box, kick, bite and grenade-punt rabbits, feral cats, feral pigs, cane toads and city foxes, dodge emu stampedes, cassowaries and crocodiles, take on the saltwater crocs of Darwin Harbour, swim, and take on a boss at the end of every world. The quokkas' cheerful Smile Squad helps out along the way, and between worlds the story plays out in little cutscenes. Every marsupial brings its joey along, and its pouch saves a spare power-up for later. Pests drop coins: spend them at the platypus's shop on silly weapons, armor and upgrades. Or settle who's the champion in a **Tournament Duel**. More worlds are on the way.

## Coins, weapons and the platypus's shop

Knocked-out pests and smashed crates drop **coins**, and they fly straight to whoever did it. Some lie around the map too. Spend them at **the platypus's shop**: SHOP on the title screen, the big SHOP button on the world map (or press B there; you come back to the same spot), and the Shop line after every map, win or lose. The platypus can't enter the tournament (egg-layers can't!), so it sells to those who can. Your coins and gear are saved in your browser, and everyone has their own: player 2 on the same keyboard, and every friend on Wi-Fi.

| Weapon | Coins | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Shanghai Slingshot | 40 | steady pebble shots that knock pests back |
| Snag Shooter | 80 | barbecue sausages that bounce along and bowl pests over |
| Thong Flinger | 110 | flings three rubber thongs (flip-flops) at once |
| Gumnut Gatling | 140 | hold SHOOT for a storm of gumnuts |
| Cricket-Ball Bowler | 200 | a rock-hard ball that goes through three pests |
| Full-Blast Garden Hose | 250 | hardly hurts, but shoves pests right back |
| Eucalyptus Flamethrower | 300 | short range, roasts a whole crowd |
| Drop-Bear Launcher | 420 | lobs a drop bear (a made-up Aussie legend) that mauls everything nearby |
| Magpie Swooper | 600 | a magpie that swoops the nearest pests by itself |

- You carry the weapon you chose in the shop. It refills by itself when you stop shooting for a moment. **Upgrade** it twice (half its price, then its full price again) for more damage and more shots.
- Weapons found in a map are yours to borrow until they're used up; then your own comes back.
- The shop has five sections, each saying what its gear is for: **Weapons**, **Armour**, **Health**, **Grenades** and **Extras**.
- **Armour** takes the hits before your health does and is mended every map and checkpoint: Bark Vest (60 coins, 25 armour), Billycan Plating (180, 60), Corrugated-Iron Suit (380, 110) and Roo-Bar Armour (650, 170). Bark patches found in maps add a little.
- **Health:** Bigger Heart (100, 220, 400 coins): +20 health each time, for every marsupial. Gum-Leaf Tonic (120, 260): heals you slowly once you haven't been hit for 4 seconds.
- **Grenades:** Grenade Belt (70, 150): an extra grenade at the start of every map. Bigger Bang (90, 200): bigger blasts that throw pests further.
- **Extras:** Joey Snacks (80, 180): your joey is ready to help again sooner. Coin Magnet (120): coins lying around fly to you from twice as far.
- **Selling back:** changed your mind? The platypus buys anything back for half of what you paid (a weapon with its upgrades, one level of an upgrade at a time). Press SELL twice to be sure.
- In the shop: move with the arrows (or A D W S), up from the top row to pick a section, buy or use with F or Enter, sell with X, and go down to PLAY. Two players on one keyboard: P1 uses A D W S, F and X, P2 the arrows, the comma key and the full stop. On phones, tap a section and an item, then BUY or SELL under your coins.
- **Duels stay fair:** shop gear doesn't come into the ring. Supply drops there bring weapons to borrow and armor for whoever grabs them.

## Ways to play

- **PLAY:** pick a marsupial, then walk the Marsupial World map (arrows or A/D) and press Enter at a stop. Clearing a map opens the next one; cleared maps show a tick and your best score, and you can replay any of them. The platypus's shop is a stop on the map too, and the SHOP button (or B) opens it from anywhere on the map. **2 PLAYERS:** two players on one keyboard, each picking their own.
- **Worlds:** The Outback (4 maps), The Rainforest (3), Cane Fields & Coast (4), Tasmania & the Alps (3), The City (4), the Time Rift bonus world (3) and Grin Isle (4), each ending with a big boss. In the water, press jump to swim up (Paddles, the water opossum, swims fastest). Some native animals have been tricked into fighting you: knock them out and they come to their senses and run home (no blood for them).
- **TOURNAMENT DUEL:** you against the CPU (JOEY, CHAMPION or LEGEND) or 2 players on one keyboard. First to win 2 rounds. Fair fights: everyone starts equal, and grenades, the gatling and power-ups only come from supply drops.
- **WI-FI:** co-op through the maps (the host picks the map, and everyone sees the world map), or a battle for 2-4 players with CPU fighters to fill the gaps (see below).

## Playing together on home Wi-Fi

2 to 4 players, each on their own computer, phone or tablet, all on the same home Wi-Fi.

1. On the computer with the game folder, double-click `share-on-network.command`. A window opens and shows the address for everyone else, like `http://<wifi-network-address>:8020`.
   - The first time, macOS may ask whether Python can accept incoming network connections. Click **Allow**, or nobody else can connect.
2. **The host** (on that same computer) opens `http://localhost:8020`, chooses **WI-FI**, then **HOST CO-OP GAME** or **HOST BATTLE**. The screen shows a 4-digit **join code**. The host changes their marsupial with the up/down arrows; in a battle, **C** adds a CPU fighter and **X** removes one.
3. **Everyone else** opens the address from the window, chooses **WI-FI**, then **JOIN A GAME**, types the code, picks a marsupial (left/right) and presses **I'm ready!**
   - **Phones:** hold them sideways. Use Safari on an iPhone (Chrome on an iPhone first needs "Local Network" allowed in the iPhone's Settings).
4. The host presses **START**. Friends can also join a game that has already started: in co-op they drop in next to the team, in a battle they jump in at the next round.

Good to know:
- If a phone loses the Wi-Fi for a moment, it rejoins by itself as the same player.
- Only one window can host. Hosting in a second window stops the first one (it says so).
- The host's pause menu has **Back to Lobby** (same code, same players) and **End Wi-Fi Game**.
- Only the game files are shared, read-only, and sharing switches itself off after 3 hours. Use it only on your own home Wi-Fi, not on public Wi-Fi.
- The online page has no WI-FI button: Wi-Fi games need the game folder and `share-on-network.command`.

## Controls

| | Player 1 | Player 2 | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D | ← / → | D-pad / stick |
| Jump | W | ↑ | A |
| Block (hold) | S | ↓ | Down / LB |
| Attack | F | , (comma) | X |
| Big attack | E | . (period) | Y |
| Special | H | / (slash) | B |
| Shoot (hold) | R | ; (semicolon) | RB |
| Grenade | G | ' (quote) | LT |
| Joey (send it out) | Q | [ | Back / View |
| Pouch (use the saved item) | T | ] | RT |
| Pause | Esc / P | Esc / P | Start |

Playing alone? Arrows with J (attack), K (big), L (special), ; (shoot), ' (grenade), [ (joey) and ] (pouch) work too. On phones and tablets, touch buttons appear during play (BOOM is the grenade). The title screen's CONTROLS button and the pause menu show this table too.

**Settings** (on the title screen): music and sound-effects volume, blood (on, gore, or confetti), difficulty for the adventure (easy, normal or hard; duels always stay fair), bigger touch buttons, high contrast, the key hints, and **changing the keys** for player 1 and player 2 (the key hints and the Controls page follow your keys).

**Tips:** some marsupials (Boomer, Chompers, Bins, Dead Fred) chain their attack: press it three times for a combo. Hold block when something charges at you, and Boomer's Tail Counter kicks back by himself. The grenade is the fun part: press the grenade key to pull the pin and drop it, then kick it with attack (short), big attack (a long lob) or the grenade key again. The special needs a full blue bar. Watch out: rabbits eat any power-up they reach first!

**Joeys and the pouch:** every marsupial carries a joey, in the pouch or riding on the back. It hops out by itself to nip pests that get close and to fetch pickups, and the joey key sends it on a Joey Rush (then it needs a rest: watch the ring by JOEY on the screen). Once per map, if you're knocked out, your joey gets you back up. Clear a map and that marsupial's joey grows (up to level 5), so it hits harder. Pick up a heart at full health, or a power-up while another is running, and it goes in the pouch: press the pouch key when you need it. In duels every joey starts at level 1 and there's no rescue.

## Privacy

The game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it loads nothing from other websites.
- Settings and progress are saved only in your own web browser (local storage) and never leave your device.
- Wi-Fi games stay on your home network. Button presses, game updates and each player's gear and coin count go between the players' devices through the host computer, are kept in memory only, and are gone when sharing stops. Coins you pick up in a friend's game are saved on your own device.
- The online version is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub sees ordinary visit information (such as IP addresses); see GitHub's privacy statement. The game itself adds nothing to that.

## Content

Cartoon action: kicks, bites, bonks, silly sound effects and cartoon weapons. **Cartoon blood is on by default.** For younger players, **Blood: OFF** in Settings turns it into confetti; **GORE** adds extra cartoon splatter. Native animals tricked into fighting never bleed: they come to their senses and run home.

The animals are real species drawn as cartoons, and the animal facts in the game are true. All characters, places and stories are made up. The game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any company, show, game, government or wildlife organisation.

## For developers

Plain HTML5 canvas and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies: every picture and sound is made in code. The plan is in `docs/` (start with `docs/README.md`; it has story spoilers). Run the tests with `tools/run-tests.sh` (macOS). A **level editor** is in `tools/editor.html`: double-click it to open any map (or a new one), place platforms, pests, crates, coins, decor, animals, gates and water, test-play it, and export it to paste into `js/data/levels.js`. `tools/make-itch-zip.sh` packs the game for an itch.io HTML page.
