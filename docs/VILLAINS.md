# Villains, bosses, hazards and guests

## The twist: the quokkas (allies who turn traitor)

The owner's idea: **the quokkas live on an island, look happy, but are evil.** The owner added on 13 Nov 2026: **they appear as allies** through most of the campaign, and not until the late levels do they reveal themselves as **traitors in a dark alliance with the reptilians**.

- **Fact:** the quokka is nicknamed "the happiest animal" for its smiley face. Most live on Rottnest Island, where tourists queue for quokka selfies.
- **As allies (worlds 1–5):** the tournament is "proudly sponsored by Grin Isle Holidays", fronted by a jolly quokka who is secretly the Grand Grinner. After the invasion, the quokkas' **Smile Squad** helps the champions: hearts, tips, supply drops and directions on the world map.
- **Their island:** the made-up **Grin Isle**, a sunny holiday island with souvenir shops, selfie spots, an evil lighthouse and a reptile lair hidden underneath. Don't name Rottnest in the game: it's a real, protected reserve.
- **Clues before the reveal,** for sharp-eyed players: shed snake skins and scaly footprints near pest camps, crates stencilled "GRIN ISLE", crocodile eyes in the river in world 3, a quokka helper chatting with a goanna in the background (both freeze when you look), and quokka help that always arrives just too late.
- **Cutscenes for the players (14 Nov 2026):** "MEANWHILE..." scenes after each world show the sponsor's secret deal with the reptilians' leader and each step of the plan (`plot1`-`plot5` in `MG.STORY`); see `DESIGN.md`.
- **The betrayal (world 7):** a victory party on Grin Isle is a trap. The quokkas drop their smiles and the reptilians come out of hiding. The quokkas shipped the pests in for the reptilians, in return for a share of Marsupial World: **Operation Marsupial World Conquest**.
- **Mechanic, the Hypnotic Grin** (from world 7): quokka minions flash their smile. A player who faces them for too long gets dizzy and slow, so players learn to turn away or block.
- **Boss, the Grand Grinner:** a giant quokka on a throne of souvenir plushies. Phases:
  1. Selfie flash: blinding camera flashes.
  2. Summons every pest boss back for a moment.
  3. Drops the smile, and it's furious. That's the punchline.

Keep it creepy-cute, not scary. Quokkas are real, threatened animals, so the ending should have a light, forgiving note: for example, they're banned from the tournament but allowed back next year if they behave.

## The reptilians (the quokkas' dark allies)

Real Australian reptiles as cartoon villains. They're native animals, so the ending is forgiving too: they go back to sunbaking. They're animals, not the "lizard people" conspiracy theory. **Cane toads are amphibians, not reptiles,** so they stay in the pests' army.

The facts below are well known but were added on 13 Nov 2026, after the planning sheet: double-check any of them before showing it in the game.

| Reptile | True fact | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Saltwater crocodile** | The largest living reptile | A river hazard whose eyes watch you in world 3 (a clue). After the reveal, heavy tank enemies. Their leader, **the Croc Emperor**, is the final boss in the lair under Grin Isle |
| **Perentie** (a goanna) | Australia's largest lizard, over 2 m long | Big brutes; the quokkas' bodyguards |
| **Lace monitor** (a goanna) | Climbs trees and raids nests | Drops on you from trees |
| **Inland taipan** | Has the most toxic venom of any land snake, though it's shy and rarely meets people | Fast venom strikers; Dead Fred shrugs off the poison |
| **Death adder** | Wiggles the tip of its tail like a grub to lure prey | Ambushers: a wriggling "snack" in the grass is a trap |
| **Frilled lizard** | Spreads a big frill around its neck and hisses when threatened, and can run on its hind legs | Sprinters; the raised frill blocks hits from the front |
| **Thorny devil** | Drinks water through tiny grooves in its skin that lead to its mouth | Spiky: hurts to punch, so kick it or use weapons |

Idea for the Time Rift: **Megalania**, an extinct giant goanna bigger than a Komodo dragon, as a monster the reptilians try to recruit. Check its size before using it: estimates vary.

## The pests (the villains everyone sees)

Real invasive species in Australia, shown as cartoon villains. The quokka–reptile alliance ships them in as its army. Over-the-top cartoon action is fine, but no realistic pest-control methods (baits, traps or culling).

| Villain | True fact | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Cane toad** | Brought to Australia in 1935 to eat cane beetles; poisonous, and has spread across the north | World 3 boss **Cane Toad King**; poison clouds (Dead Fred is immune); allied quolls must avoid them |
| **European rabbit** | Two dozen rabbits released in 1859 grew into plagues that stripped the land bare | Swarms that eat your power-ups; world 1 boss **Warren Warlord**; the bilby's arch-rivals |
| **Red fox** | Brought over for hunting in the 1800s; preys on small marsupials | Fast hunters; world 5 boss **Foxy Roxy** (urban foxes are real) |
| **Feral cat** | One of the biggest threats to small native mammals | Stealthy ambushers that hide in long grass; world 2 boss **Big Tom** |
| **Feral pig** | Digs up wetlands and forests | Charging tank enemies |
